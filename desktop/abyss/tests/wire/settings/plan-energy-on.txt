plan
energy powerd=1 ac=max battery=min from-profile=0
steps
1. set powerd_enable="YES" in rc.conf
   $ sysrc -f /etc/rc.conf powerd_enable=YES
2. set powerd_flags="-a max -b min" in rc.conf
   $ sysrc -f /etc/rc.conf powerd_flags=-a max -b min
3. onerestart the powerd service
   $ service powerd onerestart
